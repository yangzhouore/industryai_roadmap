# Editorial policy

## Coverage rules
Include substantial industrial AI research, deployments, robots, engineering simulation, production optimizations, energy/grid industrial applications, partnerships and verified industry announcements. Exclude generic AI chat apps, celebrity AI discussions and unrelated general technology news.

## Quality
- No unsupported quantitative performance claims, invented citations, or fabricated source URLs.
- Separate marketing statements from independently validated results.
- Explain why the story matters to industrial workflows; mention limitations.
- Avoid headline inflation and overuse of one company.
- Clearly label editorial opinion/analysis versus sourced fact.
- Every lead story has inspectable source references; low-quality evidence blocks the lead slot.

## Structure
Issue title, date, 1–3 lead stories, optional quick bites, per-story source links, source index and bilingual text.

## Validation
Pydantic JSON schemas, URL validation, cross-language entity/numeric checks, sensible length ranges, exact claim-to-source mapping and publication state gating.
