# Product requirements

## Product
Industrial AI Digest: bilingual, concise, source-grounded daily intelligence covering AI deployed in real industrial settings.

## Reference behaviors
Minimal editorial homepage, dated editions, 3 feature stories (maximum), quick bites, transparent ranked sources, archive, search, methodology, changelog, privacy, RSS, EN/ZH parity. Do not copy copyrighted articles, branding or artwork.

## Target audience
Industrial engineers, automation teams, AI researchers, technology analysts, manufacturing and energy professionals.

## Editorial scope
- Manufacturing AI
- Robotics & Physical AI
- Digital Twins & Simulation
- Energy & Infrastructure AI
- Industrial Agents & Copilots
- Industrial Edge AI
- Industrial AI Business & Policy

## Differentiators
- Separate research, prototype, pilot, deployed, scaling, commercial maturity.
- Identify organizations, facilities, technologies, projects and metrics.
- Flag vendor-reported versus independently verified claims.
- Allow fewer than 3 lead stories when source quality is insufficient.

## Must-have routes
`/[locale]`, ` /[locale]/digest/[slug]`, ` /[locale]/digest/[slug]/sources`, ` /[locale]/archive`, ` /[locale]/search`, ` /[locale]/methodology`, ` /[locale]/whatsnew`, ` /[locale]/privacy`; locale is `en` or `zh`.

## Explicit non-goals for MVP
User login, complex knowledge graph, embeddings/vector search, paid plans, real-time dashboards and arbitrary web scraping.
